FAQ

How do I play?

Click ▶ Play Game in the menu. It opens in a new tab and runs in your browser. No account needed.

Is it free?

Yes, Scrap Detective is completely free to play.

The game is slow to open. Why?

The game server can go to sleep when nobody is playing. Wait up to a minute and refresh the page.

What is the minimum age?

You must be at least 14. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's consent before sending us personal data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug or share an idea?

Use the feedback form or email us.

I found a copy of the game or its story somewhere else.

Please email us a link, what was copied, and your contact details. See Section 7 of the Copyright Notice.
