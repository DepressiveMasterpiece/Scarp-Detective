Links. The website may link to other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

4. How Long We Keep Data, Security and Children

Retention. We keep form responses and emails only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them, for example until the Game's release updates are sent or your feedback is dealt with. We delete your data within 30 days of a valid request, or when it is no longer needed, whichever is sooner. Server logs kept by Render are held for the period set by Render.

Security. Access to our accounts is limited to the two partners. We protect our hosting, form and repository accounts with strong passwords and keep credentials private. No system is perfectly secure, but if a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Children. You must be at least 14 years old to play. For anyone under 18, the DPDP Act requires a parent or guardian's consent before we collect personal data. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to agree to this policy before you submit any personal details. We do not track children, monitor their behaviour or show them targeted ads. If we learn that we hold a child's data without proper consent, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at to access or delete a child's data.

5. Your Rights and Our Grievance Contact

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Know and access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • Correct and update: have wrong or incomplete data fixed;
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data once it is no longer needed;
  • Withdraw consent: stop us using your data at any time;
  • Get help: have your complaint heard through our grievance contact;
  • Nominate: name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

To use any of these rights, email from the address you used with us, saying what you want. We will answer within 30 days.

We may update this policy, for example if we add analytics, accounts or paid features. The latest version will be posted on the website with a new date, and we will ask for fresh consent where the law requires it.

This policy is governed by the laws of India, and any dispute is subject to the courts at Jabalpur.
