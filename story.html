The Story So Far

The Prologue follows one day in Freaky's life, from a beating to his first honest wage.

SCENE 1

The Fruit Shop

After a stormy night, Freaky is beaten by a shop owner and driven away.

SCENE 2

The Old Bridge

Hurt and crying, Freaky chooses: drink water, or look for food (and beg).

SCENE 3

Forgiveness

He reflects, admits possible fault on both sides, and chooses to forgive.

SCENE 4

The Old Drunk Beggar

Freaky spots the man who took his home, watch and hat, and vows to reclaim them later.

SCENE 5

A Better Plan

Begging is worthless. A frail kid can't do heavy labour, so quests it is.

SCENE 6

The Guild

An empty guild, a lifeless clerk and a quest board: boars, wood, or flowers?

SCENE 7

Marin vs Marim

An old farmer teaches the difference, and Freaky ties flowers into his shirt.

SCENE 8

Return

For the first time, money won't depend on luck.
