THE LEGEND OF KR2

Core Features of the Game:

  • Game Setting & Protagonist: Set in the mysterious world of Rakuan, the game stars a 13-14-year-old orphan named Rumpaul Blumpkin, who goes by the nickname "Freaky".
  • Complex Character Traits: Freaky is driven by selfish and greedy desires, yet possesses an underlying capacity for mercy and kindness.
  • Dramatic Opening Event: Waking up after a stormy night, Freaky is beaten by an angry man and driven away from a local fruit shop.
  • Interactive Choice System: Shape the story early on with choices as fundamental as deciding whether to look for fresh water or search for food.
  • Dynamic Begging Mechanic: Attempt begging to earn quick coins—though repeated failures leave Freaky questioning his own appearance and worth.
  • Forgiveness & Moral Arc: Reflecting on his struggles while resting under a bridge, Freaky faces the emotional choice to forgive the man who attacked him.
  • Personal Vendettas & Past Conflicts: Track down an old drunk beggar who evicted Freaky from his previous shelter to reclaim his stolen watch and hat.
  • Adventurer Quest Selection: Realizing begging is ineffective and manual labor is impossible due to his frail build, Freaky must take on low-paying adventurer quests to survive.
  • Multi-Dimensional Narrator Guidance: Guided by an enigmatic, multi-dimensional narrator who introduces players to the secrets of Rakuan.
